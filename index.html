Stamp Effect
RAISED (Traditional Stamp)
The CNC carves away the background material, leaving your design standing proud above the surface. When pressed into leather, the design creates a pressed-in impression in the leather.
RECESSED (Negative / Embossing Die)
The CNC carves the design itself into the material, creating a hollow cavity. When pressed against leather, the design forces the leather to rise up and stand out in the shape of your design (like a bas-relief).

Notes
NestWorks C500 Tips
• Work area: 230 × 213 × 100mm — keep stamps within this
• Use Delrin or brass for best leather stamp results
• Enable MQL for brass/aluminum dies — extends tool life
• The ATC lets you rough with 1/8" then finish with a V-bit automatically
• ±0.02mm precision → very fine detail is achievable
• RAISED = design stands proud; presses an impression into the leather
• RECESSED = hollow cavity; forces leather to rise up in shape of design
• V-bit 60° gives the crispest leather impressions
• Ball nose for smooth depth/gradient work
⚠ Always verify in Fusion 360 or NestWorks SmartCAM before cutting
