Stamp Effect
RAISED (Traditional Stamp)
The CNC carves away the background material, leaving your design standing proud above the surface. When pressed into leather, the design creates a pressed-in impression in the leather.
RECESSED (Embossing Die)
The CNC carves the design itself into the material, creating a hollow cavity. When pressed against leather, the leather is forced to rise up and stand out in the shape of your design (bas-relief).
